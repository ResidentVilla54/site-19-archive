SCP-8472

Item #: SCP-8472

Object Class: Euclid

Special Containment Procedures: SCP-8472 is to be kept in a standard storage locker at Site-19. Access requires approval from at least one (1) Level 3 researcher. Personnel are advised to view the attached footage only with Level 3 clearance or higher, and only while a Class-B amnestic is on standby. Any recording made of SCP-8472 is to be catalogued and stored in Archive 8472-B.

Description: SCP-8472 is a ████████████ that manifests exclusively when observed through audiovisual media. Subjects exposed to SCP-8472 report a persistent sense of familiarity, followed by ██████████████. Effects are not known to persist beyond ██ hours.

SCP-8472 was recovered on ██/██/20██ following reports of anomalous activity near ████████. Further information is available to personnel with appropriate clearance.

Addendum 8472-A: Following Incident 8472-12, the footage is to be treated as a memetic hazard. All viewing is to be logged.
